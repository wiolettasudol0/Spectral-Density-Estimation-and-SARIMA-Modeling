This behavior is typical for non-stationary time series. We observed no clear isolated peaks corresponding to stable periodic behavior. Therefore, the periodogram of the raw series does not provide reliable evidence of seasonal cycles and should be interpreted with caution.

The ACF and PACF plots of the first-differenced series show a substantial reduction in long-range dependence compared to the original series. Based on the analysis of ACF and PACF plots, spectral estimates, residual diagnostics and the Ljung–Box test, the ARIMA(0,1,2) model was identified as the most appropriate for the data. This model effectively removes autocorrelation from the residuals, which behave approximately as white noise, and the Ljung–Box test does not indicate significant remaining dependence. Higher-order models do not provide a meaningful improvement in fit, therefore we selected ARIMA(0,1,2) as the final model for forecasting.
